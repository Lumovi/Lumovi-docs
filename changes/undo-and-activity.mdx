Every change Lumovi makes is confirmed by a notification, and recorded in the **Activity** log. Many can be taken back with one click.

## Notifications

When a change is done, a notification at the bottom right says what happened, in plain words: "Scaled storefront to 5 replicas", "Cordoned worker-2".

- A notification stays for six seconds, or ten when something went wrong, and eight when an [AI assistant](/assistants/approvals)'s change wasn't made because nobody approved it in time, or the assistant withdrew it. Up to three show at once; a new one makes the oldest go.
- It stays as long as the pointer is over it, or it has the focus, so you have time to read it and act.
- Some notifications have a button: **Undo**, or **Open** for something new, like the job **Run now** started, the objects **Create from YAML** made, what a [view's action](/changes/actions#custom-resources) created, or the address of a new [port forward](/debug/port-forwarding).

## Undo

**Undo** in a change's notification makes the opposite change. It's a change like any other: it has its own command, and its own line in the Activity log.

| Change | What Undo does |
| --- | --- |
| Scale | Scales back to the previous number of replicas |
| Cordon, Uncordon | Uncordons, or cordons, the node again |
| Pause rollout, Resume rollout | Resumes, or pauses, the rollout again |
| Suspend, Resume | Resumes, or suspends, the CronJob or Job again |
| Change image | Puts the previous images back |
| Edit replica range | Puts the autoscaler's previous range back |
| Edit labels | Puts the previous labels and annotations back |
| A view's action | Applies the undo the view spells out, when it has one. Actions that create an object have none. |
| **Apply…** in [Right-sizing](/metrics/right-sizing) | Puts the workload's previous requests and limits back: "Restored the resources of checkout" |

If the undo fails (someone changed the object in between, say), a notification says "Couldn't undo", with the reason.

Restarts, rollbacks, drains, deletes, YAML edits, [changes to several objects at once](/changes/bulk) and changes an [AI assistant](/assistants/approvals) asked for have no Undo. Restarts and rollbacks can be followed by another rollback, from **Roll back…**. See [Actions by kind](/changes/actions).

## The Activity log

The **Activity** button in the header (a clock with an arrow) opens **Changes this session**: every change you made from Lumovi, newest first. A count on the button shows how many are new since you last looked.

Each entry shows:

- **What happened**, like "Restarted checkout". Choose it to open the object, when it's in the cluster you're looking at.
- **When**, and the cluster, when it's another one than you're looking at.
- **Its status**: In progress, Done, Failed (with the reason), or Rejected.
- **Who asked**, for a change an [AI assistant](/assistants/overview) asked for: "via Claude Code", or "rejected, asked by Claude Code" for one you rejected, with the note you wrote.
- **The kubectl command** that does the same thing, with **Copy command**.

The log covers actions, YAML edits, objects created from YAML, changes to several objects at once, right-sizing, undos and Helm changes. **Clear** empties it.

<Note>
  The log lives only as long as the session: it's empty again when you restart Lumovi, or reload the page when it runs in your cluster. For a lasting record of who changed what, use the cluster's audit log.
</Note>

### Changes AI assistants asked for

A change an [AI assistant](/assistants/overview) asked for gets a line too, once it's over: made after you approved it, or failed, also when it would no longer have done what you saw; made without asking, where the cluster [allows that](/assistants/approvals#changes-they-ask-for); or rejected. Its notification says whose it was: "As Claude Code asked, in demo." Changes nobody approved in time, and ones the assistant withdrew, have only a notification. In the cluster's audit log, they're yours: Lumovi makes them with your credentials. On a [server](/assistants/server), each of your open pages gets the line, and the server's log records it too. See [Approving changes](/assistants/approvals).
