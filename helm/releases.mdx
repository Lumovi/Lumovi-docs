**Helm releases** lists every release in the cluster, or in the namespace you picked. Open it from the sidebar, or press <kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>H</kbd>.

Lumovi reads releases from where Helm itself keeps them, so looking needs no `helm` on your computer and no access beyond reading Secrets. Changing a release goes through your own `helm`: see [Upgrade and roll back](/helm/upgrade-and-rollback).

## The list

Each release shows its **Status**, **Chart** and version, **App version**, **Revision** and when it was **Updated**. Sort by any column; sorting by **Status** puts failed releases first. Press <kbd>/</kbd> to filter by name, namespace, chart or app version, or narrow the list by status with the chips:

| Chip | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| <span className="lumovi-status healthy">Deployed</span> | The release is running its latest revision. |
| <span className="lumovi-status progressing">Pending</span> | An install, upgrade or rollback is under way: **Pending install**, **Pending upgrade**, **Pending rollback**. |
| <span className="lumovi-status warning">Uninstalling</span> | Helm is removing it. |
| <span className="lumovi-status critical">Failed</span> | The last install, upgrade or rollback failed. |
| <span className="lumovi-status neutral">Other</span> | Anything else, like **Uninstalled** for a release kept with its history. |

The list refreshes every 15 seconds. **Install chart** at the top installs a new one: see [Install a chart](/helm/install).

## A release

Click a release to open it next to the list. Its actions (**Upgrade…**, **Roll back…** and **Uninstall…**) are at the top.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Overview">
    The facts: **Chart** and its version, **App version**, **Revision**, **Last change** (Helm's description of it, like "Upgrade complete"), when it was **Installed** and **Updated**, what the chart says it is, and its **Subcharts**. Below them are the release's **Notes**, as `helm install` printed them.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Resources">
    Every object the release's manifest makes, with its live status from the cluster, so you can see at a glance whether what the chart made is healthy. An object the manifest lists that isn't in the cluster shows as <span className="lumovi-status warning">Missing</span>. Click any object to open it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Values">
    The values the release runs with, two ways:

    - **Set for this release**: only what was set at install or upgrade, like `helm get values`.
    - **With the chart's defaults**: the chart's own `values.yaml` with yours on top, merged the way Helm does (maps merge, anything else replaces, and `null` removes a key), like `helm get values --all`.

    **Copy values** copies what's shown.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Manifest">
    The YAML the release applied, as Helm rendered it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="History">
    Every revision, newest first, with its description, chart and app version, age and status. Pick any two with **From** and **To**, and compare their **values** or their **manifest**, with lines added and removed counted.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Releases Flux manages

If a release is managed by [Flux](https://fluxcd.io)'s helm-controller, Lumovi says so at the top of the release: Flux puts back changes made to it any other way. **Open its HelmRelease** takes you to the `HelmRelease` object, where you can reconcile, suspend or resume it with Lumovi's [view for Flux](/custom-resources/built-in-views#flux). The upgrade, rollback and uninstall dialogs warn you too.

## What it needs

| To | Your account needs |
| --- | --- |
| See releases | `list` on Secrets (and ConfigMaps), in the namespace or cluster-wide |
| Change them | Whatever the chart's objects need, since your own `helm` makes the change with your credentials |

Helm 3 and 4 keep each revision of a release in a Secret labeled `owner=helm` (or a ConfigMap, with Helm's `configmap` storage driver). Lumovi reads both. If your account can't list Secrets, the page says so instead of showing an empty list.
