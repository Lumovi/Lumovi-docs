A plugin with code doesn't draw its page. It describes it, as parts Lumovi has, and Lumovi draws them: the same tables, tiles and cards its own pages are made of, in its own type, colors and spacing, on the desktop and on a phone. Its code runs in a [sealed box](/custom-resources/plugins#pages-drawn-by-a-plugin’s-code), and everything it says passes through Lumovi's door, which checks each part against what's on this page.

This is the reference for the parts, for what a page can ask of Lumovi, and for its limits. To start a plugin of your own with code, use the [template](https://github.com/Lumovi/Lumovi-plugins/tree/main/template) in Lumovi-plugins, and see [Your own plugins with code](/custom-resources/plugins#your-own-plugins-with-code). What a plugin's file can say is in the [plugin format](/reference/plugin-format).

## What a page is

- **A page is made only of Lumovi's parts.** Where you need one Lumovi doesn't have, ask for it in an [issue](https://github.com/Lumovi/Lumovi/issues): a plugin can't draw its own. There's no HTML, no CSS and no canvas, and no width, gap or position is a plugin's to give.
- **Every text is drawn as text.** A name, a word, a line or a server's error is the plugin's, and Lumovi draws it as its characters: never as a link, a class or markup, and a character that can't be seen, or that would turn a line round, shows as a placeholder.
- **Levels and icons are Lumovi's.** A status is one of `healthy`, `progressing`, `warning`, `critical` and `neutral`, with the plugin's word beside it. An icon is one of the [icons](/reference/plugin-format#icons) a view picks from.
- **Times are said by Lumovi.** A moment is given as RFC 3339 text, and Lumovi says how long ago or how soon, and keeps it current.
- **What's pressed opens Lumovi's own.** A row, a card or a tile that opens something names an object Lumovi handed the page, or a list of a kind its file says its pages [open](/reference/plugin-format#spec), and Lumovi opens it.

## The parts

Three arrangements hold the rest, and nothing else does:

| Part | Holds | Is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Stack` | Any part but a `Split` | One under another: what a page is when nothing else is said |
| `Pair` | Exactly two `Card`s | Two cards side by side, `even` unless `first: wider` (seven parts of twelve). One under the other on a narrow page |
| `Split` | One `Stack` | A pick list at its side, then a stack that follows what's picked. A page has one at most, and nothing holds it. Narrower than 640 pixels, it's two pages: the list, or **Back** above the picked one's |

And the parts they hold:

| Part | Takes | Is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Header` | `name`, `status` (a level, a word, a `detail` line), up to 8 `buttons` | What's picked, its status, and what can be done with it. Three buttons show, the rest behind **…** |
| `Tiles` | 2 to 4 `tiles`: a `key`, a `label`, an `icon`, a `value`, a `detail` line with a `level`, `opens` | A row of headlines, of equal width. A value is a `figure` ("2/3"), `words` ("Never") or a `time` |
| `Card` | `title`, `note`, `about`; holds one `Table`, `Graph` or `Counts` | One part under a title, with a quiet note at its right |
| `Table` | `title`, up to 8 `columns`, `headings`, `rows`, `shows`, `state` | Rows with a lead (a name, a word beside, a line under, a level) and a cell for each column. Rows may hold rows, one level, and stand in groups |
| `Graph` | `title`, `legend`, `columns`, `nodes`, `edges`, `state` | Cards with lines from one to another, solid or dashed. Past 300 cards or 600 lines, it's shown as its table, and says why |
| `Counts` | Up to 6 `groups`, each a `title` and up to 50 `rows` of a `value` and a `count` | Shares of a whole, as bars, the largest first |
| `Logs` | `title`, `pod`, `container` | A pod's logs in Lumovi's own log view, where the file says [`logs`](/custom-resources/plugins#logs-on-a-plugin’s-page). The plugin's code never sees a line |
| `Secret` | `title`, `secret`, `keys` | A Secret's keys, each hidden until **Reveal**, with **Copy**, in Lumovi's own card, where the file says [`showsSecrets`](/reference/plugin-format#spec). The plugin's code never sees a value |

### Every part, exactly

What each part takes, as Lumovi's door checks it. A property not listed here stops the page, as a part Lumovi doesn't have does. Properties starting with `on` are what the page is told: a row opened, a button pressed, a pick.

| Part | Holds | Takes | Needs | Tells the page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Table` | nothing | `columns`, `cut`, `headings`, `leadHeading`, `nameSorts`, `onOpen`, `onRetry`, `rows`, `shows`, `state`, `title` | `columns`, `headings`, `rows`, `title` | `open`, `retry` |
| `Graph` | nothing | `columns`, `edges`, `legend`, `nodes`, `onOpen`, `onRetry`, `state`, `title` | `columns`, `edges`, `nodes`, `title` | `open`, `retry` |
| `Tiles` | nothing | `onOpen`, `tiles` | `tiles` | `open` |
| `Header` | nothing | `buttons`, `name`, `onPress`, `status` | `name` | `press` |
| `Split` | `Stack` | `list`, `onPick` | `list` | `pick` |
| `Card` | `Table`, `Graph`, `Counts` | `about`, `note`, `title` | `title` | nothing |
| `Pair` | `Card` | `first` | nothing | nothing |
| `Counts` | nothing | `groups` | `groups` | nothing |
| `Stack` | `Tiles`, `Header`, `Card`, `Pair`, `Table`, `Graph`, `Counts`, `Logs`, `Secret` | nothing | nothing | nothing |
| `Logs` | nothing | `container`, `pod`, `title` | `title` | nothing |
| `Secret` | nothing | `keys`, `secret`, `title` | `title` | nothing |

### A table's cells

A column has a `name`, a `kind`, a `width` (`narrow`, `normal`, `wide` or `text`) and whether it `sorts`. A cell's kind is its column's:

| Kind | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `text` | Words. `mono` sets them in fixed-width type, and `full` is the whole of what they stand for (a commit's whole hash behind its first seven), said in the tooltip |
| `number` | A number, or one with its unit as text ("7 nodes") |
| `age` | A moment, which Lumovi says as how long ago |
| `status` | A level and its word. Sorts worst first |
| `meter` | `used` of `of`, with its text ("24 of 64 cores"). Without `of`, there's no limit |
| `none` | Nothing to say: a dash |

In place of rows, a table (or a graph, or a pick list) can give a `state`: `busy` while it reads, `failed` with a title and what the cluster said, and **Try again**, `empty` with a title and a line, or `quiet`, one calm line. Where a table's rows come from a read Lumovi cut at its limit, the table names that read's `cut`, and Lumovi says so above the rows, from its own numbers.

### A Secret, shown and not held

Where a plugin's file says its pages show a Secret that its own kind names ([`showsSecrets`](/reference/plugin-format#spec)), a `Secret` part shows it: Lumovi's own card, captioned "Shown by Lumovi. Toys can’t see it." for a plugin named Toys, with a row for each key, hidden until **Reveal**, and **Copy** without revealing.

- **It takes a token, never a Secret.** `secret` is the token Lumovi handed the page with the object that names the Secret, at the path the file declares. `keys` can only narrow the keys the file names: one outside them stops the page. A token the page doesn't hold shows a card that says "This object names no Secret.", and nothing is read.
- **Lumovi reads it as you, on your press, and never before.** Each read is in the [audit log](/audit/events#access-access) as `secret.read`, naming the plugin.
- **Nothing goes back to the plugin's code.** The part holds nothing and tells the page nothing: not a value, its length, or that it was revealed, copied or refused.
- **Only on a page whose code Lumovi handed over.** Anywhere else, the same part shows its title and no Secret.
- Where your organization's [policy](/desktop/policy#pluginsecrets) says `pluginSecrets: off`, the card says "Your organization’s policy doesn’t let plugins show what’s in Secrets.", and nothing is read.

A page has at most 20 of them, each with at most 20 keys. None of the plugins Lumovi comes with shows a Secret yet.

### A card's `about`

A card can say where its data comes from, when its title and note can't say it: up to 240 characters, shown quietly under the title, two lines at most, the rest in its tooltip. Under a finger, where there's no tooltip, it wraps whole. Put what matters most first: on a narrow card, two lines hold about 60 characters.

### A header's buttons

A button has a `key` and a `name`, and may say:

- **`changes: true`**: the button changes something. Only its press lets the page propose a change, which waits for the person in Lumovi's dialog. A proposal after any other press is refused. Of a header's buttons, the last that changes something is drawn last, filled; one that only reads or goes never is. A phone doesn't draw a button that changes something, and says where it is.
- **`asks: true`**: it asks before it does anything, so its name ends in "…".
- **`note`**: what it does, up to 120 characters, under the buttons.
- **`off`**: why it can't be pressed now, up to 80 characters. It's drawn off, with this as its tooltip. Under a finger, the reason stands under the buttons, before the note, as "Back up now is off: A backup is running."
- **`goes`**: where it goes, in place of telling the page it was pressed: one of Lumovi's own places for an object the page was handed (a pod's **Logs** or **Shell** tab, **Port forward**), where the file's [`links`](/custom-resources/plugins#links-to-lumovi’s-own-places) say so, or a list of Lumovi's own. It changes nothing and asks nothing first.

## What a page can ask for

A page's code asks Lumovi, and Lumovi reads as the person, within their access:

- **A list of a kind, or one object**: its own kinds whole, and of Kubernetes' own only the kinds Lumovi lets a plugin read (Pods and Nodes), each only the fields its file's [`reads`](/reference/plugin-format#spec) names. A list can be picked by labels and by the fields Lumovi picks a kind by, and can ask for each object's live usage. Lumovi keeps a list up to date, and hands each object with a token: what the page names when a row opens it.
- **A pod's last log lines**, once, where its file says `logs: read`. See [Logs on a plugin's page](/custom-resources/plugins#logs-on-a-plugin’s-page).
- **A path of its tool's own server**, once, where its file says `server`. See [A plugin's own server](/custom-resources/plugins#a-plugin’s-own-server).
- **A change**, proposed after a press of a button that says `changes: true`: of its own kinds, as its file's `changes` say. See [Changes a page proposes](/custom-resources/plugins#changes-a-page-proposes).

A read Lumovi refuses is answered with why, in a word and a sentence of Lumovi's, and the page goes on. An ask that isn't written as one, or that goes past a limit, stops the page.

## What a page can't do

Its code runs in Lumovi's sealed box, where it has:

- no network: nothing reaches out, whatever it tries;
- no WebAssembly, and no more code: no workers, no `import()`, no `eval`;
- no `crypto.subtle`, in any browser or on the desktop: hashing, signing and verifying are Lumovi's. Random numbers are there, from `crypto.getRandomValues`;
- no storage that another plugin, another of its pages, or Lumovi can reach;
- nothing of Lumovi's page, its cookies or its credentials.

A plugin of your own may be at most 2 MB of code.

## Limits

A page past any of these is stopped, and says why in its place. The rest of Lumovi goes on.

| What | At most |
| --- | --- |
| Parts and texts a page holds | 5,000, 32 deep, 1,000 in one part |
| A text | 4,000 characters; a name, a word or a cell 253, a line 500 |
| Everything a page holds | 2 MB |
| A message to the door | 1 MiB, and 2,000 steps in one commit |
| In a second | 20,000 steps, 60 commits and 10 asks |
| A table's rows | 5,000, with 200 inside one row, and 8 columns |
| A graph | 5,000 cards and 10,000 lines |
| A pick list | 1,000 rows |
| Lists kept up to date | 8 by a page, 16 by all of a plugin's pages |
| Reads going at once | 50 by a page, 100 by a plugin, 20 not yet answered |
| Secrets shown | 20 `Secret` parts on a page, with 20 keys in one |
| Logs shown at once | 4 by a page, 8 by a plugin: more wait their turn |
| Asks of its server not yet answered | 2 by a page; 4 by a plugin, and more wait their turn |
| A refresh | Once in 10 seconds: one asked sooner isn't made |

In the desktop app, Lumovi also stops a page that uses more memory than a plugin may, or more than half of a processor core over half a minute.

## What needs which Lumovi

Everything on this page comes with Lumovi 1.22.0, the first with plugins' pages. A plugin's `needs.lumovi` is at least the first Lumovi that carries everything it uses: the checks in Lumovi-plugins read that from the Lumovi they pin, and refuse a plugin whose file says an older one.
